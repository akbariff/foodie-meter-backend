About FoodieMeter

Better awareness, one meal at a time.

FoodieMeter is an Android-oriented dietary and nutrition recommendation application designed to help users set calorie goals, record meals, manage recipes, discover curated content and monitor dietary progress.

Important: FoodieMeter provides general dietary guidance and is not a replacement for professional medical advice.
